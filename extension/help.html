AllenPlan Help

This page opens when you first install AllenPlan. To reopen it at any time, click the AllenPlan extension icon and choose Help.

Import your degree audit

  1. Open DARS and sign in to MyPlan with your UW account.
  2. Select your major (Computer Science, Computer Science: Data Science, or Computer Engineering) and open the completed audit.
  3. With the audit on screen, click the AllenPlan extension icon. Check the major shown in the popup, then choose Import DARS. The planner opens.
Open DARS

Generate a schedule

Choose your starting quarter, year, and maximum CS courses per quarter. Add any desired courses, then select Generate schedule. The planner saves your preferences as you edit.

Use or replace a saved audit

Choose Open planner in the popup to use your saved audit. To replace it, open the updated audit in DARS and choose Reimport DARS in the popup.

Delete imported audit removes the saved audit and clears the schedule. Your planner preferences are kept.
